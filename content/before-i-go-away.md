# Before I Go Away

_Original URL: https://ikengala.squarespace.com/home/beforeigoaway_

[![Before I Go Away, handwritten title (links to the YouTube video)](../assets/images/before-i-go-away/hero-cutout.png)](https://youtu.be/2_dct1jkoIw?si=Oq4PZ6AVF3oIRgYl)

*Before I Go Away* (released March 2025) is the clearest proof-of-concept in the IkeN catalog, not because it was the most meticulously planned campaign, but rather because it wasn’t. The track generated organic TikTok virality a month after release, then did so again roughly a year later, with each moment requiring a different analysis of what was happening in real time. 

This case study demonstrates my ability to recognize audience signals in real time, and convert them into durable growth, geographic reach, catalog plays, and audience retention.

### Trend-Responsive Content Creation

During each campaign cycle, TikTok was the main attention driver. Once the track started gaining traction on TikTok, I produced and posted follow-up content built around the same sound, keeping the project inside the platform’s existing momentum rather than competing against it with unrelated content.

Furthermore, I created content centered around existing trends and pop media in order to attract attention, converting fans of other media to fans of the song (specifically, targeting FilmTok & BookTok).

### Data-Driven Geographic Targeting

Used streaming and social analytics to identify which regions were actually driving engagement, then weighted content and promotional push toward those markets, turning a random spike into a more deliberate, regionally informed growth strategy.

### Catalog Conversion Funnel

Treated *Before I Go Away* as an entry point rather than simply as an endpoint, actively directing new listeners toward the back catalog, converting single-song discovery into sustained listening and following, effectively turning passive listeners into fans.

## Outcomes

**Reach**

Due to two distinct viral cycles, the track now sits at **250K+** total streams. The first cycle took the song from a 10K stream baseline with approx. 300 daily, to an average of 800 daily streams. The second cycle, starting from an **80K baseline**, peaked at **5K daily streams** (525% increase) before settling at a sustained **1K daily average.**

**Engagement Signals**

At peak, the track generated **3000 saves in a single day with 600+ playlist adds**, with a **6.3 streams-per-listener ratio** across the spike month, meaning new listeners were not just streaming the song, but replaying and moving across the catalog.

**Geographic Spread**

The viral surge concentrated in **South Africa, the UK, the US, France, Kenya, and Saudi Arabia**, and critically, these same markets now form the core of the ongoing catalog audience,  successfully converting a temporary spike into a lasting listener base.

**Cross-Platform & Catalog Growth**

Gained 300+ new followers during the spike. On Apple Music, **daily average listeners are up 431% and plays up 462% year-to-date**, even measured after the peak (which itself reached 1,300 daily plays/400 daily listeners, up from a baseline of 50 plays/30 listeners). On YouTube, growth exceeded the platform’s reporting cap of 999%, with daily plays having risen from 30 daily at the start of the year to a peak of approximately 1600, and is currently sustained at 250.

**UGC & Organic Amplification**

Beyond owned content, the track was also independently used as a sound in **450+ user-generated TikTok videos**; distribution and reach were generated entirely by other creators, with zero paid promotion or coordination on my end.
